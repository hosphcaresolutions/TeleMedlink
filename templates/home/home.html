About TelemedLink

Your trusted partner in accessible healthcare

Our Mission

TelemedLink was founded with a simple goal: to make quality healthcare accessible to everyone in Kenya. We believe that distance should never be a barrier to receiving excellent medical care.

Our Story

Established in 2025 by a team of healthcare professionals and technology experts, TelemedLink has grown from a small startup to a trusted healthcare platform serving thousands of patients across Kenya.

Our Team

We are proud to work with over 10 certified doctors and healthcare specialists who are committed to providing compassionate, personalized care to every patient.
